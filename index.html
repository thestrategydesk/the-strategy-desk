Our Proposition
01 Who We Help

Who We Help

The Strategy Desk works with professionals and organisations looking to strengthen financial and strategic capabilities through practical, real-world learning.

Our primary audience is students and early-to-mid-career professionals, including analysts, associates and managers.

  • Corporate teams

    Strengthening financial understanding, commercial judgement and strategic thinking across finance, M&A, strategy and other business functions. Training helps participants assess business performance, evaluate investment decisions and approach strategic challenges with greater confidence.

  • Universities & educational organisations

    Bringing practical, industry-led perspectives into academic learning, bridging the gap between theory and professional practice.

  • Professional training providers

    Complementing existing educational programmes with specialist expertise, practical teaching and real-world examples.

  • Individuals

    Developing technical expertise, analytical skills and confidence in applying financial concepts through tailored learning.

02 Offerings

Offerings

The Strategy Desk delivers practical training courses across finance, valuation, financial markets and strategic analysis. Courses combine technical foundations with real-world examples and case studies, helping participants understand key concepts and apply them in practice.

Core subject areas include:

  • Financial statement analysis and business performance
  • Company, industry and market analysis
  • Company valuation, including DCF, NPV and trading and transaction multiples
  • Corporate finance, M&A and investment appraisal
  • Financial markets, bonds and credit analysis
  • Commercial and strategic thinking

Courses are structured around these subject areas, with topics, depth and emphasis adapted to participants' experience and learning objectives. A course can focus on selected topics or combine several areas into a broader programme.

Delivery formats

Courses can be delivered virtually or in person, with formats adapted to the audience and learning objectives.

  • Corporates

    Focused workshops and structured development programmes tailored to team roles, experience and business objectives.

  • Universities & educational organisations

    Guest lectures, workshops, specialist modules and case-study sessions that complement existing curricula.

  • Professional training providers

    Specialist teaching, topic-specific learning materials and practical case studies integrated into existing programmes.

  • Individuals

    One-to-one sessions focused on specific technical topics and professional development objectives.

03 Our Approach

Our Approach

What makes our approach different is the emphasis on real-world application. We use live companies, earnings releases, transactions, market developments and case studies to bring concepts to life.

Our sessions combine technical fundamentals, structured problem-solving and real-world application, helping professionals translate learning into greater impact in their roles.

Alongside technical capability, we also focus on developing professional judgement: how to ask better questions, challenge assumptions, and assess the credibility of data, analysis and output. As information and analysis become increasingly accessible through AI and other tools, these skills are becoming an even more important differentiator.

Sessions include knowledge checks to reinforce learning and help participants apply concepts with confidence.

About

About The Strategy Desk

Our aim is to help professionals build stronger technical foundations, develop a structured approach to analysis, and strengthen the commercial judgement needed to excel in their roles and advance their careers.

Drawing on more than ten years of experience across audit, strategy consulting and M&A, we combine real-world industry experience with a passion for teaching and developing talent.

Our experience includes training at a Big Four accounting firm, qualifying as a Chartered Accountant (ACA), and progressing into Director-level strategy and M&A leadership roles.
